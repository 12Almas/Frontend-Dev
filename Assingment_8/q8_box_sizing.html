Comparing box-sizing

box-sizing: content-box; (Default)

The width (250px) only applies to the content. The padding and border are added *outside* of that width.

Total Width:
250px (width) + 40px (padding) + 20px (border) = 310px

box-sizing: border-box;

The width (250px) includes the content, padding, and border. The browser shrinks the content area to fit.

Total Width:
The box is exactly 250px wide, as specified.
